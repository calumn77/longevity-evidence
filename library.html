How to interpret the ratings

Three separate questions

01 · Research stage

Has the approach been investigated in cells, animals or humans? A Phase 1 trial is primarily an early step in testing safety—not confirmation of benefit.

02 · Confidence in benefit

How convincingly do studies show a meaningful human health improvement? We consider controls, sample size, consistency, endpoints and follow-up.

03 · Demonstrated outcome

Is the result a molecular marker, a physiological measurement, a clinical improvement, or longer life? These outcomes must not be treated as interchangeable.

Our editorial principle

A changed biomarker is not necessarily improved health. Improved health in mice does not establish benefit in humans. A treatment entering a human trial does not mean it is an established rejuvenation therapy.
